MOONFOREST — ORIGINAL CREATURE AUDIO R1 — 2026-11-17

New wolf, fox, boar and bear effects were synthesized specifically for
MoonForest from oscillator excitation, seeded noise, resonant filters and
original envelopes. No animal recording, commercial game sound, stock clip,
prior MoonForest sound or third-party sample was used in these new cues.
The alpha-wolf score is an original note arrangement rendered with original
synthesized instrumental tones, not a borrowed song, sampled recording or
an imitation of a named artist. No Runway or other hosted generation service
was used. Editable generators, score and MIDI are preserved with the sources.

Production: MoonForest project, created with Codex assistance.
Methods are documented in make_creatures.py and compose_alpha.py.
Tool software: Python, NumPy, SciPy, FFmpeg; no GeneralUser GS samples used.

This attribution applies to the newly created creature cues and alpha-wolf
score only. Existing bear/Elena/world tracks retain their separate original
licenses and attribution. Original synthesis does not guarantee that an
automated matching system will never issue a false match.
